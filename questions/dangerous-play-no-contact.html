LAW 12 · Fouls & misconduct · 2026/27

A player attempts a high kick near an opponent’s head. The opponent pulls back to avoid injury, and there is no contact. The referee stops play. What restart applies?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AIndirect free kick to the opponent’s team ✓
  2. BDirect free kick
  3. CPenalty kick automatically
  4. DDropped ball

THE DECISION

Indirect free kick to the opponent’s team

Playing in a dangerous manner without contact is an indirect-free-kick offence. Contact or another separate offence could change the decision.
