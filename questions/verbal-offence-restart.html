LAW 12 · Fouls & misconduct · 2026/27

While the ball is in play, a player commits a verbal offence against an opponent and the referee stops play. What is the restart?

THE OPTIONS

  1. ADirect free kick
  2. BIndirect free kick ✓
  3. CDropped ball
  4. DThrow-in

THE DECISION

Indirect free kick

All verbal offences are penalised with an indirect free kick. Disciplinary action depends on the nature of the words.
