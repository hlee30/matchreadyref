LAW 8 · The Start and Restart of Play · 2026/27

While the ball is in play, an attacking player takes a shot at goal. The ball passes the defending goalkeeper and suddenly bursts within the goal area. As a result, it remains on the field of play but does not enter the goal. What is the correct decision?

See Decision ↓

THE DECISION

The referee stops play and replaces the defective ball. Play is then restarted with a dropped ball for the defending team’s goalkeeper, as the ball was in their penalty area when play was stopped.
