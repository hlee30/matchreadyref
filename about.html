ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Study the moment.
Understand the Law.

Match Ready Ref is an independent practice site for football referees and learners. It presents Law 8 match situations as questions with revealable answers and direct links to the official 2026/27 Laws of the Game.

How to use it

Take a mixed practice round, reveal each answer, then follow the official source for decisions you want to review. The question bank is readable without JavaScript, including by search crawlers and assistive technology.

Scope

The scenarios assume the 2026/27 Laws of the Game and ordinary 11-a-side play unless a question says otherwise. Some protocols, competition options, and local rules vary. Check the competition regulations before applying a decision in a match.
