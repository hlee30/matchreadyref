LAW 8 · The Start and Restart of Play · 2026/27

Play is restarted with a dropped ball for Team A. After that, the ball touches the ground and leaves the field of play, without touching a player. What is the correct decision?

Show answer ↓

THE DECISION

The referee drops the ball again for Team A player.
