LAW 12 · Fouls & misconduct · 2026/27

An opponent blocks a goalkeeper who is trying to release the ball from the hands. The goalkeeper is still holding it. What is the restart if the referee stops play for the opponent’s interference?

THE OPTIONS

  1. ACorner kick against the goalkeeper
  2. BIndirect free kick for the goalkeeper’s team ✓
  3. CPenalty kick
  4. DRetaken goal kick

THE DECISION

Indirect free kick for the goalkeeper’s team

An opponent may not prevent a goalkeeper from releasing the ball from the hands. If play is stopped for that offence, award an indirect free kick to the goalkeeper’s team. A caution depends on the opponent’s conduct; it is not automatic.
