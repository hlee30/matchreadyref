LAW 8 · The Start and Restart of Play · 2026/27

An attacking team player has possession of the ball in the defending team’s penalty area. Play is stopped by the referee (no offence committed). How is play restarted?

See Decision ↓

THE DECISION

The referee will restart the game with a dropped ball for the defending team goalkeeper.
