LAW 11 · Offside · 2026/27

An attacker receives the ball directly from a teammate’s corner kick while closer to the goal line than the second-last opponent. What is the offside decision?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AOffside offence
  2. BNo offside offence ✓
  3. COffside only if the attacker scores
  4. DCorner kick retaken

THE DECISION

No offside offence

There is no offside offence from receiving the ball directly from a corner kick. Normal offside considerations apply after a subsequent teammate touch.
