LAW 8 · The Start and Restart of Play · 2026/27

Play is restarted with a dropped ball for Team A and a player kicks the ball before it touches the ground. Is this allowed?

See Decision ↓

THE DECISION

No, because the ball is not in play until it touches the ground. If the ball touches the player first (before touching the ground), the referee drops the ball again for Team A.
