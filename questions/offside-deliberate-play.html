LAW 11 · Offside · 2026/27

A defender controls an opponent’s pass and deliberately tries to clear it, but miscues the clearance to an attacker who was in an offside position when the opponent passed. There was no save. What is the decision?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AAlways offside because the attacker was ahead
  2. BPlay on; the deliberate play resets the offside assessment ✓
  3. CDropped ball
  4. DCaution the defender

THE DECISION

Play on; the deliberate play resets the offside assessment

A controlled, deliberate play by the opponent can reset offside even if the clearance is poor. A mere deflection or deliberate save would be different.
