LAW 8 · Restarts · 2026/27

Play is stopped while the ball is inside the penalty area and there is no other restart. To whom is the dropped ball given?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AThe team that last touched it
  2. BThe defending team’s goalkeeper ✓
  3. CThe attacking captain
  4. DTwo players contest it

THE DECISION

The defending team’s goalkeeper

If play was stopped with the ball inside the penalty area, the dropped ball is for the defending team's goalkeeper in that penalty area. Only that player participates.
