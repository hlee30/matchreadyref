LAW 16 · Restarts · 2026/27

A goal kick is unfairly delayed. The referee signals the five-second countdown and the ball is still not in play when it ends. What is awarded?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AIndirect free kick in the goal area
  2. BCorner kick to the opponents ✓
  3. CGoal kick to the opponents
  4. DRetaken goal kick

THE DECISION

Corner kick to the opponents

Under the 2026/27 countdown rule, the opponents are awarded a corner kick when a delayed goal kick has not put the ball in play by the end of the count.
