LAW 10 · Shoot-outs · 2026/27

During a penalty shoot-out, a kicker accidentally touches the ball with the non-kicking foot immediately after kicking it. The ball does not enter the goal. How is the kick recorded?

THE OPTIONS

  1. ARetaken
  2. BMissed ✓
  3. CSaved
  4. DCancelled and taken by another player

THE DECISION

Missed

In a shoot-out, this accidental double touch is recorded as a miss if the ball does not enter the goal; if it enters, the kick is retaken.
