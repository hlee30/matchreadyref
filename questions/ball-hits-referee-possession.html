LAW 9 · Ball in play · 2026/27

The ball strikes the referee, stays on the field, and the team in possession changes because of that contact. What is the restart?

THE OPTIONS

  1. APlay on in every case
  2. BDropped ball ✓
  3. CIndirect free kick
  4. DThrow-in

THE DECISION

Dropped ball

Play is stopped for a dropped ball when a ball touching a match official remains on the field and changes possession, among other listed outcomes.
