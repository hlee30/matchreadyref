LAW 11 · Offside · 2026/27

An attacker is level with the second-last opponent when a teammate passes forward. The attacker runs beyond that opponent, collects the pass, and scores. What is the decision?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AGoal ✓
  2. BIndirect free kick for offside
  3. CDirect free kick for the defenders
  4. DRetake the pass

THE DECISION

Goal

Judge the position when the teammate plays the ball. Being level with the second-last opponent at that moment is onside; moving ahead afterwards does not create an offside offence.
