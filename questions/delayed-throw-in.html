LAW 15 · Restarts · 2026/27

After the referee signals a five-second countdown for an unfairly delayed throw-in, the team still has not taken it at the end of the count. What happens?

THE OPTIONS

  1. ASame team retains the throw-in
  2. BThrow-in awarded to the opponents ✓
  3. CCorner kick
  4. DIndirect free kick

THE DECISION

Throw-in awarded to the opponents

The opponents receive the throw-in. A caution is only required if the player excessively delays the restart after possession has changed.
