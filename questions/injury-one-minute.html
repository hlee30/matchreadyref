LAW 5 · Referee decisions · 2026/27

An outfield player receives routine on-field assessment after play is stopped for the injury. No listed exception applies. When may the player re-enter?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AImmediately at the restart
  2. BAfter play restarts and one minute has elapsed, with the referee’s permission ✓
  3. COnly at half-time
  4. DWhenever their team has possession

THE DECISION

After play restarts and one minute has elapsed, with the referee’s permission

The 2026/27 off-field treatment protocol normally requires the player to leave, wait until play has restarted and one running minute has elapsed, and receive the referee’s signal to re-enter. Exceptions include certain serious injuries and injuries from an opponent’s cautionable or sending-off offence.
