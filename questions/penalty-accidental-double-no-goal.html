LAW 14 · Penalty kicks · 2026/27

At a penalty kick, the ball immediately touches the kicker’s non-kicking leg by accident and misses the goal. What is the restart during normal play?

THE OPTIONS

  1. ARetake the penalty kick
  2. BIndirect free kick to the defending team ✓
  3. CGoal kick
  4. DDropped ball

THE DECISION

Indirect free kick to the defending team

When the accidental immediate double touch does not produce a goal during normal play, the defending team receives an indirect free kick. A shoot-out has a separate recorded-as-missed outcome.
