LAW 3 · Players · 2026/27

In an ordinary match with no applicable temporary dismissal or special protocol, one team is reduced to six players. May the match continue?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AYes, until half-time
  2. BYes, if both captains agree
  3. CNo ✓
  4. DOnly if that team is winning

THE DECISION

No

A match may not start or continue with fewer than seven players, subject to specific exceptions involving temporary dismissals and approved protocols.
