LAW 16 · Restarts · 2026/27

A goal kick travels directly into the kicker’s own goal without any other touch. What is the restart?

THE OPTIONS

  1. AOwn goal
  2. BRetaken goal kick
  3. CCorner kick to the opponents ✓
  4. DDropped ball

THE DECISION

Corner kick to the opponents

A goal kick can score directly only against the other team. If it directly enters the kicker’s own goal, award the opponents a corner kick.
